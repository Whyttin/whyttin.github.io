✨this✨is✨Bewhiskered!✨

I have a friend who always does a reel recapping the conventions they go to and it just felt appropriate given the amount of time I got to spend with old and new friends alike this past Bewhiskered. Since there was no parade this year I broke down and did something I had been planning to do for awhile which was set up an impromptu photoshoot and I could not have asked for a better turn out, to go from thinking one moment that nobody is going to show up to sending friends inside to get me water because I'm having to direct so many people that I can't step away, really made me realize what wonderful people I have around me in my life.

I've said it multiple times in the past couple days but I really do want to send a heartful thank you out to those who I am very grateful to have in my life, those who are always my test dummies, who bring me the aforementioned water, who show up when I call upon them - I could not do what I do without the support of everyone I have in my life, thank you so, so much!
