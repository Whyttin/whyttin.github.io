Welcome home to all that I saw over the weekend at Anthrocon - I have to admit, I had such an incredible time for being convinced to come somewhat last minute, glad I gave AC another go.

Even though the Omni room we were in had messed up air con the first couple days, Condados easily made up for it, that plate of nachos is way more food than I expected for the $12 or so that it costs - also, shoutout to the cornerstore for having my latest vice (salted caramel java Monsters lol).

No parade and no block party means a little less photos than I was expecting, but that just means that I got to spend a little more time present in the moment, and give me a bit of an editing break where I know I'll have my hands full with Megaplex next month.
